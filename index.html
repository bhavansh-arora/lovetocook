27 layers.
Zero shortcuts.

A proper croissant takes three days. This is what you'll do with your own hands in the Croissant Masterclass.

Croissant Masterclass
  1. 3Lock-in

    A cold slab of butter

    French butter wrapped in a soft détrempe dough. Dough, butter, dough.

  2. 9First fold

    Roll, fold in three

    Every letter fold triples the layers. Too warm and the butter melts in.

  3. 9Rest & roll

    Chill, then roll thin

    The dough relaxes and the butter stays in perfect, unbroken sheets.

  4. 27Second fold

    Twenty-seven layers

    In the oven, steam lifts every layer apart. That's the shatter and the honeycomb.
