The craft, up close

Butter. Fold. Repeat.

A croissant isn't kneaded, it's folded. Every fold in three triples the sheets of butter, until twenty-seven of them puff apart in the oven.

Learn it in the Croissant Masterclass
  1. Step 01 · Lock-in

    A cold slab of butter

    French butter wrapped in a soft détrempe dough. Three layers: dough, butter, dough.

  2. Fold ×3
    Step 02 · First fold

    Roll, fold in three

    Every letter fold triples the layers. Temperature is everything: too warm and the butter melts in.

  3. Chill
    Step 03 · Rest & roll

    Nine thin sheets

    Rest, chill, roll again. The dough relaxes and the butter stays in perfect, unbroken sheets.

  4. Fold ×3
    Step 04 · Second fold

    Twenty-seven layers

    In the oven, steam lifts every layer apart. That's the shatter, the honeycomb and the sound of a proper croissant.
